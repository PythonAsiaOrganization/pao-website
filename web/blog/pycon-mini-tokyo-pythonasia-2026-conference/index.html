PyCon mini Tokyo with PythonAsia conference — December 12, 2026

PyCon JP and the Python Asia Organization (PAO) will co-host a new collaboration event: PyCon mini Tokyo with PythonAsia conference, on Saturday, December 12, 2026, in Shibuya, Tokyo.

PyCon JP published the announcement first, and we recommend reading it directly: 【2026年12月12日開催】新イベント「PyCon mini Tokyo with PythonAsia conference」のお知らせ. This post is PAO's side of the same news.

What It Is

A one-day Python conference built around one idea: exchange between Japan's Python community and the communities of the rest of Asia (and the world in fact). It runs in two tracks — one in Japanese, one in English — so locals and international visitors get equal footing in the program.

This is the first event we have co-hosted. We previewed it at our open space at PyCon JP 2026 in Hiroshima, and it is now confirmed with a date and a venue.

At a Glance

  • Date: Saturday, December 12, 2026
  • Time: 10:00 – 19:00
  • Venue: GMO Internet Group SHIBUYA FUKURAS, 16F Event Space, 1-2-3 Dogenzaka, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo
  • Tracks: Japanese and English

Keynote

PAO will be inviting two keynote speakers who both work on security at the Python Software Foundation:

  • Seth Larson, Python Security Developer in Residence, PSF — working on CPython, PyPI, and ecosystem-wide security.
  • Mike Fiedler, PyPI Safety and Security Engineer, PSF — over thirty years in software and systems, now focused on keeping PyPI safe.

Their session is planned as one keynote slot shared by both speakers.

Registration

Registration has not opened yet. It opens October 1, 2026, at 09:00 (JST) on connpass, and closes on the day of the event.

  • General: ¥1,500 (food and drink, paid at the door), 150 spots
  • Student: ¥500 (food and drink, paid at the door), 20 spots
  • Patron: ¥10,000 (paid at the door), 10 spots

All tickets are first-come, first-served. The connpass event page is live now:
